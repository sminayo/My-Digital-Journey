My Childhood Journey

Growing up in Mukuru kwa Reuben

I was born on 5 of December 1998 and raised in the vibrant Mukuru kwa Reuben community in Nairobi. My early years were filled with laughter, family gatherings, and the rich culture of our neighborhood.

Favourite childhood memory

One of my favorite pastimes was playing "Hide and Seek" with my friends, a traditional game that taught me teamwork and strategy.

Family Support and Values

My family instilled strong values in me, including perseverance, honesty, and kindness. These principles continue to guide me today.

Every weekend, we would gather to share stories, celebrate milestones, and support each other through challenges. These moments created an unbreakable bond within my family.

I also loved listening to my grandmother’s tales of resilience and hope, which inspired my dreams for a better future.
